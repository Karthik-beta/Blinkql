Query big files without uploading them

Blinkql is a SQL workbench that runs entirely in your browser, for files too big for a spreadsheet and too sensitive to upload. It's being built in the open. An early version works today, but it's still slow to show large results.

A replay of the workbench with synthetic factory data. An early version of the real workbench is ready to try.

For the exports a spreadsheet can't open

Machine logs, breakdown records, attendance sheets and stock counts often arrive as exports with a million rows or more. Spreadsheets struggle to open them, and what's inside is often too sensitive to upload. Blinkql opens the file in your browser tab and lets you ask questions of it in SQL.

Queries run in your browser
The engine runs inside the tab, on your computer. There's no server doing the work.
Your files never leave your computer
The browser reads the file directly. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no account.
Built for exports of 1–2 million rows
The size of a month of machine data from a plant, or a full stock ledger.

Work in progress

Blinkql is being built in the open. The work is split into eight milestones, each ending with a deployed app. The first two are done: the workbench now opens a file of a million rows and queries it in your browser. It's deliberately simple for now, so large results are slow to show. The current milestone, , , makes it fast.

Try the early workbench, see how fast it is today, read the roadmap or follow the milestones on GitHub.
